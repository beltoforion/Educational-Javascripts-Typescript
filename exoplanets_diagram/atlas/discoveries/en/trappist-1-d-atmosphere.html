TRAPPIST-1 d has no Earth-like atmosphere

TRAPPIST-1 d is the third planet of the system 40 light-years away and lies on the edge of the temperate zone. It orbits its star in only four days. Using its NIRSpec instrument, the James Webb Space Telescope found no sign of molecules that are common in Earth's atmosphere, such as water, methane or carbon dioxide.

The research team therefore rules out an Earth-like atmosphere. It remains open whether the planet has an extremely thin atmosphere like Mars, is covered by thick, high-altitude clouds like Venus, or is a barren rock with no atmosphere at all.

Source: ESA/Webb: "Webb narrows atmospheric possibilities for Earth-sized exoplanet TRAPPIST-1 d." (weic2516), 2025-08-13
