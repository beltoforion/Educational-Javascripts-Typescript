July 11, 2013

HD 189733 b: first measurement of an exoplanet's color

The STIS spectrograph on the Hubble Space Telescope was used to determine the color of an exoplanet for the first time. The researchers observed the system before, during and after HD 189733 b disappeared behind its star. The total brightness dropped only in blue light, so the planet mainly reflects blue.

The color does not come from an ocean: silicate particles in the hazy atmosphere probably scatter the blue light. The gas giant, 63 light-years away, orbits very close to its star. Its atmosphere is hotter than 1000 degrees Celsius, and winds reach about 7000 kilometers per hour.

Source: ESA/Hubble: "Hubble spots azure blue planet", 2013-07-11
