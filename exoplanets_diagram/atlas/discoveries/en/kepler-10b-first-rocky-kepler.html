January 10, 2011

Kepler-10b: the Kepler mission's first rocky planet

NASA's Kepler mission confirmed its first rocky planet. Kepler-10b measures 1.4 times the diameter of Earth, which made it the smallest planet known outside the solar system at that time. Radial velocity measurements at the Keck Observatory confirmed the planet; it has 4.6 Earth masses and an average density of 8.8 grams per cubic centimeter.

Kepler-10b orbits its star in just 0.84 days and is more than 20 times closer to it than Mercury is to the Sun. It therefore does not lie in the habitable zone.

Source: NASA: "NASA's Kepler Mission Discovers Its First Rocky Planet." (Release 11-007), 2011-01-10
