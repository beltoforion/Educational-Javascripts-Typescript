November 15, 2017

Ross 128 b: a temperate planet only 11 light-years away

Using ESO's HARPS spectrograph, a planet of about Earth's mass was discovered orbiting the red dwarf Ross 128, only 11 light-years from the solar system. Ross 128 b orbits the star every 9.9 days and was, after Proxima b, the second-closest temperate planet known.

The planet receives 1.38 times as much radiation as Earth; its equilibrium temperature is estimated at between −60 and 20 degrees Celsius. Whether it lies inside, outside or on the edge of the habitable zone is uncertain. Ross 128 is a particularly quiet red dwarf, which could improve the prospects for conditions friendly to life.

Source: ESO: "Closest Temperate World Orbiting Quiet Star Discovered." (eso1736), 2017-11-15
