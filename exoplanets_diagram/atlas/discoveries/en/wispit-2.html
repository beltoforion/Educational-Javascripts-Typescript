WISPIT 2: two forming planets in a ringed disk

Two planets are forming in the disk around the young star WISPIT 2, and both have been observed directly. WISPIT 2b was discovered in 2025: almost five Jupiter masses, about 60 times as far from the star as Earth is from the Sun. Now the SPHERE instrument on the Very Large Telescope and GRAVITY+ on the VLT Interferometer have confirmed a second planet. WISPIT 2c is four times closer to the star and twice as massive.

After PDS 70, it is only the second system with two directly observed forming planets. Both lie in gaps of the extended, ringed disk. A further, narrower gap could point to a third planet.
