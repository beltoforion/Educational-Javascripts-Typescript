TOI-849 b: the exposed core of a gas giant

TOI-849 b is about 40 times as heavy as Earth but has only 3.4 times Earth's radius. Because of this high density it must consist mainly of iron, rock and water and can contain only very little hydrogen and helium. The research team interprets it as the exposed core of a gas giant – the first of its kind.

Either the planet lost its gaseous envelope, or it failed to build up a full atmosphere in its early life. It orbits its Sun-like star, about 730 light-years away, in just 18 hours; its surface temperature is about 1,500 degrees Celsius.
