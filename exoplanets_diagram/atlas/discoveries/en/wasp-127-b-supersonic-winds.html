WASP-127 b: supersonic winds of 33,000 kilometers per hour

On the gas giant WASP-127 b, a jet stream blows around the equator at about 9 kilometers per second, or roughly 33,000 kilometers per hour. It is the fastest wind of its kind ever measured on a planet. For comparison: the fastest wind measured in the Solar System, on Neptune, reaches 1,800 kilometers per hour.

The measurement was made with the CRIRES+ instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope. The planet, more than 500 light-years away, is slightly larger than Jupiter but has only a fraction of its mass. The team also found water vapor and carbon monoxide as well as cooler poles.

Source: ESO: "Extreme supersonic winds measured on planet outside our Solar System.", 2025-01-21
