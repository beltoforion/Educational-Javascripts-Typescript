January 11, 2023

LHS 475 b: the first exoplanet confirmed by Webb

For the first time, the James Webb Space Telescope confirmed the existence of an exoplanet. Hints of LHS 475 b came from NASA's TESS satellite; the NIRSpec spectrograph needed only two transit observations for the detection. The rocky planet has 99 percent of Earth's diameter and orbits its red dwarf in just two days. It is 41 light-years away.

Whether it has an atmosphere remained open. The data rule out a thick, methane-dominated envelope like that of Saturn's moon Titan; a pure carbon dioxide atmosphere, however, would still be possible. The planet is a few hundred degrees warmer than Earth.

Source: ESA/Webb: "Webb Confirms Its First Exoplanet", 2023-01-11
