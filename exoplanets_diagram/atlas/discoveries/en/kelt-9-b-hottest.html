KELT-9 b: a planet hotter than many stars

The gas giant KELT-9 b is the hottest gas planet known so far. Its dayside reaches more than 4,600 kelvins – more than many stars. The planet has 2.8 Jupiter masses but is only half as dense as Jupiter, because the radiation from its star puffs up the atmosphere.

In 2018, researchers from the universities of Geneva and Bern detected iron and titanium in gas form in its atmosphere. KELT-9 b orbits its star, about 650 light-years away, in 36 hours; the planet may be gradually evaporating.
