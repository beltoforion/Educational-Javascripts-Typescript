Kepler-1649c: Earth-size planet found in old Kepler data

While re-examining Kepler data, researchers found a planet that a computer algorithm had previously misidentified. Kepler-1649c has 1.06 times the radius of Earth and receives from its star 75 percent of the light Earth gets from the Sun. It lies in the habitable zone.

The planet orbits a red dwarf 300 light-years away in 19.5 days. Nothing is known about its atmosphere, and the size estimate has significant margins of error. According to NASA, of all the planets found by Kepler it is the most similar to Earth in size and estimated temperature.

Source: NASA: "Earth-Size, Habitable Zone Planet Found Hidden in Early NASA Kepler Data.", 2020-04-15
