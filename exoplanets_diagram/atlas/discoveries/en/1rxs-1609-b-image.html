September 14, 2008

First picture of a likely planet around a Sun-like star

Using the Gemini North telescope in Hawaii, astronomers imaged a companion of about eight Jupiter masses next to the young star 1RXS J160929.1-210524. The infrared image, taken with the Altair adaptive optics system and the NIRI camera, shows it as a small dot to the upper left of the bright star.

The companion is about 330 times as far from the star as the Earth is from the Sun. In 2008 it was regarded as a likely planet; in June 2010 further observations confirmed that it moves together with the star, which is about 500 light-years away.

Sources: Gemini Observatory / NOIRLab: "First Picture of Likely Planet around Sun-like Star" (gemini0807)., 2008-09-14; Gemini Observatory / NOIRLab: "First Directly Imaged Planet Confirmed Around Sun-Like Star" (gemini1008)., 2010-06-29; NOIRLab: "First Picture of Likely Planet around Sun-like Star" (image gemini1008a)., 2010-06-29
