October 1995

51 Pegasi b: first planet around a Sun-like star

In October 1995, Michel Mayor and Didier Queloz announced the discovery of 51 Pegasi b, the first planet around a Sun-like star. They found it at the Haute-Provence Observatory in southern France. The gas giant, about 50 light-years away, is regarded as the archetype of the "hot Jupiters": planets similar to Jupiter in size and mass that orbit very close to their star.

In 2015, ESO's HARPS instrument made the first spectroscopic detection of visible light reflected by an exoplanet, on this very planet. The result implied a mass of about half that of Jupiter. Mayor and Queloz received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2019.
