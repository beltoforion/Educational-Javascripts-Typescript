HD 20794 d: a super-Earth in the habitable zone of a Sun-like star

After more than 20 years of measurements with the HARPS and ESPRESSO spectrographs, a third planet was confirmed orbiting the Sun-like star HD 20794. The star is only 20 light-years away and slightly less massive than the Sun.

According to the team, HD 20794 d has about six Earth masses and takes 647 days to complete one orbit. Its orbit is markedly elliptical and carries it from the outer to the inner edge of the habitable zone over the course of its year. Whether the planet could host life cannot yet be said. Because the star is so close, it is considered a worthwhile target for future telescopes.
