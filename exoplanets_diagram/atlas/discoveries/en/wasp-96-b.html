WASP-96 b: water in one of Webb's first spectra

Among the first published results of the James Webb Space Telescope, on July 12, 2022, was a spectrum of the gas giant WASP-96 b. On June 21, the NIRISS instrument observed for 6.4 hours as the planet passed in front of its star, and measured the starlight filtered through the atmosphere at 0.6 to 2.8 microns.

The spectrum shows the unambiguous signature of water as well as indications of haze and clouds that earlier observations had not revealed. WASP-96 b is about 1150 light-years away, has less than half the mass of Jupiter at 1.2 times its diameter, and orbits its star in three and a half days.

Source: ESA/Webb: "Webb Reveals Steamy Atmosphere of Distant Planet in Exquisite Detail", 2022-07-12
