March 17, 2025

Webb images 51 Eridani b and finds carbon dioxide

The NIRCam near-infrared camera of the James Webb Space Telescope has directly imaged the young exoplanet 51 Eridani b. A coronagraph blocks the starlight; in the image a star symbol marks the position of 51 Eridani, and the planet appears as a red spot to its left (light at 4.1 microns).

The observations show that the planet is rich in carbon dioxide. This is regarded as strong evidence that it formed like Jupiter and Saturn: by slowly building a solid core that attracted gas. The planet is 17.7 billion kilometers from its star; the system lies 96 light-years from Earth.
