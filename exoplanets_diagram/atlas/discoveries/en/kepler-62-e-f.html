April 18, 2013

Kepler-62 e and f: two small planets in the habitable zone

The Kepler mission found five planets around the star Kepler-62, two of them in the habitable zone. Kepler-62 f is only 40 percent larger than Earth, closer to Earth in size than any other planet known in a habitable zone at that time. Kepler-62 e orbits on the inner edge of the zone and is roughly 60 percent larger than Earth.

The orbital periods are 122 days (e) and 267 days (f). The star is smaller and cooler than the Sun and about 1,200 light-years away. According to NASA, Kepler-62 f is likely rocky; its mass and composition, however, have not been measured.
