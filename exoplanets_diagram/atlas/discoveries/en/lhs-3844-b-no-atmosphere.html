LHS 3844 b: a rocky planet probably without an atmosphere

The Spitzer Space Telescope detected light from the surface of the rocky planet LHS 3844 b. The planet is 48.6 light-years away, has 1.3 times the radius of Earth and orbits its star in just 11 hours. Its dayside is about 770 degrees Celsius hot.

Practically no heat is transported between the day and night sides. This matches bare rock without a significant atmosphere. The data rule out an atmosphere with more than ten times the pressure of Earth's. The surface could consist of dark, cooled volcanic rock, similar to the mare regions of the Moon.

Source: NASA/JPL: "NASA Gets a Rare Look at a Rocky Exoplanet's Surface.", 2019-08-19
