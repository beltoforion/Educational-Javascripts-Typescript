September 7, 2006

Hubble photographs the companion of the red dwarf CHXR 73

In February 2005 the Advanced Camera for Surveys of the Hubble Space Telescope recorded a companion of the young red dwarf CHXR 73. In the near-infrared image it is the bright dot at the lower right next to the overexposed star; it is one hundred times fainter than the star.

At twelve Jupiter masses the object lies in the border region between planet and brown dwarf; discoverer Kevin Luhman classified it as a brown dwarf. It is about 200 times as far from its star as the Earth is from the Sun. The star is about two million years old and 500 light-years away.
