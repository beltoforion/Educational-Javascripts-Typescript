July 22, 2021

Possible detection of a forming exomoon

An image taken with the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) shows the forming planetary system around the star PDS 70, almost 400 light-years away. It shows the circumstellar disk around the star with regions that have already been cleared of gas and dust by planets. Also visible is a circumplanetary disk around one of the system's exoplanets (small bright dot below the center of the image). Exomoons could form in this disk.

Source: Daniel Lingenhöhl: "Hier könnte ein Exomond entstehen." Online (in German); Spektrum.de; retrieved 2022-01-05
