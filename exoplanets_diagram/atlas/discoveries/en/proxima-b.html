Proxima b: a planet around the nearest star

Using the HARPS spectrograph on ESO's 3.6-meter telescope at La Silla, Proxima Centauri was observed regularly during the first half of 2016 in the Pale Red Dot campaign. The star alternately approaches Earth and recedes from it at about 5 kilometers per hour, in a rhythm of 11.2 days.

The cause is a planet with at least 1.3 Earth masses that orbits the red dwarf at a distance of about 7 million kilometers. Proxima b lies in the habitable zone, where liquid water would be possible. At just over four light-years, it is the closest exoplanet to the Sun.

Source: ESO: "Planet Found in Habitable Zone Around Nearest Star", 2016-08-24
