CD-35 2722: evidence for an "exomoon" around a brown dwarf

Observations with ESO's Very Large Telescope point to an object resembling a moon in the system of CD-35 2722. It does not orbit a planet but a brown dwarf of about 37 Jupiter masses, which in turn orbits a star with about half the mass of the Sun.

The object is at least as massive as Jupiter and was found with the radial velocity method: it makes the brown dwarf wobble slightly. The team calls it an "exosatellite". If the finding is confirmed, it would be the first "moon" discovered outside the Solar System – so far there are only a few candidates.
