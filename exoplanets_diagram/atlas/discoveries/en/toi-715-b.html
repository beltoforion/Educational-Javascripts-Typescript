TOI-715 b: a super-Earth in the conservative habitable zone

NASA's TESS satellite found a super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf 137 light-years away. TOI-715 b is about one and a half times as wide as Earth and orbits the star every 19 days.

The orbit lies within the so-called conservative habitable zone – a narrower definition of the region where liquid water could exist on the surface. Further conditions would have to be met for that, above all a suitable atmosphere. The same system might harbor a second, roughly Earth-sized planet; it has not yet been confirmed.
