Gliese 12 b: a temperate world between the sizes of Earth and Venus

Using NASA's TESS satellite and other instruments, two international teams discovered a planet orbiting the red dwarf Gliese 12, almost 40 light-years away. Gliese 12 b orbits the star every 12.8 days and is as large as Earth or slightly smaller – comparable to Venus.

The planet receives 1.6 times as much energy as Earth does from the Sun. Without an atmosphere, its surface temperature would be an estimated 42 degrees Celsius. Whether it has an atmosphere is still unknown. According to the discoverers, it is the nearest temperate, Earth-size transiting world and therefore a good target for the James Webb Space Telescope.
