July 2, 2018

PDS 70 b: first confirmed image of a planet in formation

The SPHERE instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope captured the first confirmed image of a planet still forming in the disk of gas and dust around its young star. PDS 70 b appears as a bright point next to the center, which is covered by a coronagraph mask. The work was led by the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy in Heidelberg.

The gas giant has a few Jupiter masses and is about three billion kilometers from its star, roughly as far as Uranus is from the Sun. Its temperature is around 1000 degrees Celsius; the spectrum points to a cloudy atmosphere.

Source: ESO: "First Confirmed Image of Newborn Planet Caught with ESO’s VLT", 2018-07-02
