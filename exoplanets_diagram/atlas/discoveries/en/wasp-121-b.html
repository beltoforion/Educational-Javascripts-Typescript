WASP-121 b: first 3D map of an exoplanet atmosphere

For the ultra-hot gas giant WASP-121 b, also known as Tylos, the three-dimensional structure of an exoplanet atmosphere has been mapped for the first time. To do this, the ESPRESSO instrument combined the light of all four Unit Telescopes of ESO's Very Large Telescope and observed one full transit.

The motions of iron, sodium and hydrogen trace winds in three layers: deep down, gas flows from the hot side to the cooler side; above that, a jet stream circles the equator. Titanium was also detected. The planet is about 900 light-years away, and one orbit takes only about 30 hours.

Source: ESO: "“Out of science fiction”: First 3D observations of an exoplanet’s atmosphere reveal a unique climate", 2025-02-18
