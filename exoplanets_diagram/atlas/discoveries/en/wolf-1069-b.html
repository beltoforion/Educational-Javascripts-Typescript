February 3, 2023

Wolf 1069 b: an Earth-mass planet in the habitable zone

A team led by the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy found, as part of the CARMENES program, a planet of roughly Earth's mass orbiting the red dwarf Wolf 1069. It orbits the star in 15.6 days at one-fifteenth of the distance between Earth and the Sun and lies in the habitable zone.

Wolf 1069 b receives about 65 percent of the radiant power that Earth gets from the Sun. Its rotation is probably locked to its orbit, so that it always turns the same side toward the star. At a distance of 31 light-years it is, according to the team, the sixth-closest Earth-mass planet in the habitable zone of its star.

Source: Max-Planck-Institut für Astronomie: "A nearby potentially habitable Earth-mass exoplanet.", 2023-02-03
