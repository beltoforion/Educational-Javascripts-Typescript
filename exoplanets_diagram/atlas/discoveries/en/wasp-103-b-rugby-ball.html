January 11, 2022

WASP-103 b: a planet shaped like a rugby ball

ESA's Cheops satellite has detected the deformation of an exoplanet for the first time: because of the tidal forces of its star, WASP-103 b is not spherical but stretched more like a rugby ball.

The planet in the constellation Hercules is almost twice the size of Jupiter, has 1.5 times its mass and orbits its star in less than a day. From the shape of the transit light curve, the team derived the so-called Love number, which describes how mass is distributed inside the planet. Data from the Hubble and Spitzer space telescopes were also used for the measurement.
