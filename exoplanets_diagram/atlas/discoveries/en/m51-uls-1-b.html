October 25, 2021

First exoplanet candidate in another galaxy

By analyzing data from the Chandra X-ray Observatory, NASA researchers have found a first exoplanet candidate in another galaxy. The candidate was discovered while searching for transit events in X-rays. It is located in the Whirlpool Galaxy and orbits a binary system (M51-ULS-1) in which a neutron star (or a black hole) pulls gas from its companion star and makes it glow in X-rays in a small accretion disk.

At present, however, it cannot be ruled out that the object is a red or brown dwarf. Nevertheless, detecting transit events in X-rays appears to be suitable in principle for discovering extragalactic exoplanets.
