April 24, 2023

Protoplanet confirmed in the disk around HD 169142

A new analysis of several years of observations with the SPHERE instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope has confirmed a forming planet around the star HD 169142. The three panels show the disk around the masked star; marked are the position of the protoplanet in 2015 and the predicted positions for 2017 and 2019.

The protoplanet has about the mass of Jupiter and carves a circular gap in the disk. It orbits the star at a distance somewhat larger than that between Neptune and the Sun and leaves a spiral wake in the disk material.

Source: ESO Picture of the Week: "A new planet is born" (potw2317a)., 2023-04-24
