January 22, 2021

The seven TRAPPIST-1 planets have almost identical densities

A new analysis delivered the most precise density measurements obtained up to then for a group of exoplanets. The densities of the seven TRAPPIST-1 planets differ by no more than 3 percent. The planets therefore probably consist of similar proportions of the same materials.

However, they are about 8 percent less dense than they would be with an Earth-like composition. Possible explanations are a lower iron content (about 21 percent instead of Earth's 32 percent), iron bound with oxygen as rust, or – for the four outer planets – a water content of about 5 percent of the mass. Which explanation applies is an open question.

Source: NASA/JPL: "The 7 Rocky TRAPPIST-1 Planets May Be Made of Similar Stuff.", 2021-01-22
