LP 890-9 c: a super-Earth at the inner edge of the habitable zone

Two super-Earths were found orbiting the cool dwarf star LP 890-9, about 100 light-years away. The inner planet, LP 890-9 b (orbital period 2.7 days), had been detected by NASA's TESS satellite. Using the SPECULOOS telescopes, a team from the University of Liège discovered the second planet, LP 890-9 c.

It is about 40 percent larger than Earth and orbits the star in about 8.5 days. This places it in the habitable zone, although close to its inner edge. Liquid water would be possible provided the planet has a sufficient atmosphere. Only TRAPPIST-1 is a cooler star with known planets than LP 890-9.

Source: Université de Liège: "SPECULOOS discovers a potentially habitable super-Earth.", 2022-09-07
