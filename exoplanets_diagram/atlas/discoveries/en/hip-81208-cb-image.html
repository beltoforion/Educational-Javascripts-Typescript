HIP 81208 Cb: planetary-mass object in a quadruple system

In archival data from the SPHERE instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope, a previously unknown object was found in the HIP 81208 system. The annotated image shows the masked central star A, the brown dwarf B and the low-mass star C; the insets show its newly discovered companion Cb.

Cb has about 15 Jupiter masses, which places it on the border between planets and brown dwarfs. According to ESO, HIP 81208 is the first hierarchical quadruple system found by direct imaging.
