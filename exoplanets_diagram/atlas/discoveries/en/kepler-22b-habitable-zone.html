December 5, 2011

Kepler-22b: Kepler's first confirmed planet in a habitable zone

NASA's Kepler mission confirmed its first planet in the habitable zone of a star. Kepler-22b has about 2.4 times the radius of Earth and orbits its star in 290 days. Like the Sun, the star belongs to spectral class G.

The planet is about 600 light-years away. Whether it consists predominantly of rock, gas or liquid was not known at the time of the announcement. Kepler-22b is therefore not necessarily a rocky planet.

Source: NASA/JPL: "NASA's Kepler Confirms Its First Planet In Habitable Zone.", 2011-12-05
