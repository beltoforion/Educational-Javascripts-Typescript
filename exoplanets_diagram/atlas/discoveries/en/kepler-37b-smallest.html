Kepler-37b: a planet barely larger than the Moon

The Kepler mission discovered a system containing the smallest planet found around a Sun-like star up to that time. Kepler-37b is only slightly larger than the Moon and has about one-third of Earth's diameter. It orbits its star every 13 days at less than one-third of Mercury's distance from the Sun.

The planet is almost certainly rocky. According to the researchers it has no atmosphere; the surface temperature is estimated at about 700 kelvins (roughly 430 degrees Celsius). The system is about 210 light-years away.

Source: NASA/JPL: "NASA's Kepler Mission Discovers Tiny Planet System.", 2013-02-20
