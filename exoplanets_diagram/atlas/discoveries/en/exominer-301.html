Artificial intelligence discovers 301 new exoplanets

Confirming exoplanet candidates is a time-consuming process in which the measurements of different detection methods are compared with each other in order to rule out false detections as far as possible. NASA researchers have now succeeded in confirming 301 new exoplanets in one go in the data of the Kepler space telescope, using a neural network called "ExoMiner".

Source: NASA: "New Deep Learning Method Adds 301 Planets to Kepler’s Total Count." Online; NASA/JPL; retrieved 2022-01-05
