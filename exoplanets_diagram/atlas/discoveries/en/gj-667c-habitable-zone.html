June 25, 2013

Gliese 667C: three super-Earths in the habitable zone

A re-analysis of ESO's HARPS data together with further measurements produced evidence for up to seven planets around the star Gliese 667C. Three of them are super-Earths in the habitable zone, where liquid water could exist – more than had been known in any system before. Five of the signals are considered very confident, the sixth and seventh tentative.

Gliese 667C has just over one-third of the Sun's mass and belongs to a triple star system 22 light-years away in the constellation Scorpius. The planets were found through radial velocity measurements.

Sources: ESO: "Three Planets in Habitable Zone of Nearby Star." (eso1328), 2013-06-25; arXiv: Anglada-Escudé et al., "A dynamically-packed planetary system around GJ667C with three super-Earths in its habitable zone.", 2013-06-25
