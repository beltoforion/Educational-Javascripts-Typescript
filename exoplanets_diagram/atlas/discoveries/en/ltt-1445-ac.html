LTT 1445 Ac: Hubble measures the nearest Earth-sized transiting planet

The Hubble Space Telescope has determined the size of LTT 1445 Ac, the nearest known Earth-sized planet that passes in front of its star. The planet has 1.07 times the diameter of Earth. Previously it was unclear whether it only grazes the star's disk, in which case its size would have been underestimated.

LTT 1445 Ac is thus a rocky planet with approximately Earth-like surface gravity. At roughly 260 degrees Celsius, however, it is too hot for life as we know it. It orbits a red dwarf in a triple star system 22 light-years away in the constellation Eridanus.

Source: ESA/Hubble: "Hubble measures the size of the nearest transiting Earth-sized planet." (heic2311), 2023-11-16
