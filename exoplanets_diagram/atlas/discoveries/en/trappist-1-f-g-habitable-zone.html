February 5, 2018

TRAPPIST-1 f and g: Hubble examines the habitable-zone planets

Three of the seven TRAPPIST-1 planets – e, f and g – lie in the habitable zone, where liquid water could exist on the surface. The Hubble Space Telescope examined planets d, e, f and g. For d, e and f the data rule out a cloud-free, hydrogen-rich atmosphere; for g this was not possible.

This supports the view that they are rocky planets rather than small gas planets. A study presented on the same day determined the planets' densities more precisely. According to it, f, g and h are far enough from the star that water on their surfaces could be frozen into ice.
