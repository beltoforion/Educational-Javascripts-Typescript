Webb studies possible moon-forming disk around CT Cha b

Using the spectrograph of its MIRI instrument, the James Webb Space Telescope has for the first time directly measured the chemical and physical properties of a possible moon-forming disk around a large exoplanet. The planet CT Cha b is 625 light-years away and separated by 74 billion kilometers from its star, which is only two million years old.

In the disk around the planet the team found seven carbon-bearing molecules, including acetylene and benzene. No moons were detected. The picture is an artist's impression, not an observation.
