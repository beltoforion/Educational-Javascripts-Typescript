October 30, 2013

Kepler-78b: Earth's size and density, but scorching hot

Kepler-78b was the first roughly Earth-sized exoplanet for which both size and mass were measured. It is 1.2 times the size of Earth and 1.7 times as massive. This yields a density like Earth's – a clear indication of a rocky planet.

It is not habitable: Kepler-78b orbits its star, about 400 light-years away, in just 8.5 hours. Two teams determined the mass using the radial velocity method, among others at the Keck Observatory. Both papers were published in the journal Nature.
