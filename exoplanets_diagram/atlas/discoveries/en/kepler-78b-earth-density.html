Kepler-78b: Earth's size and density, but scorching hot

Kepler-78b was the first roughly Earth-sized exoplanet for which both size and mass were measured. Its diameter is 1.2 times Earth's and its mass 1.7 times Earth's. This yields a density like Earth's – a clear indication of a rocky planet.

It is not habitable: Kepler-78b orbits its star, about 400 light-years away, in just 8.5 hours. Two teams determined the mass using the radial velocity method, among others at the Keck Observatory. Both papers were published in the journal Nature.

Source: NASA/JPL: "Scientists Discover the First Earth-size Rocky Planet.", 2013-10-30
