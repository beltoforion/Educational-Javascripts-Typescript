February 22, 2017

TRAPPIST-1: seven Earth-sized planets around a dwarf star

At least seven planets, all about the size of Earth, orbit the dwarf star TRAPPIST-1, only 40 light-years away. TRAPPIST-1 b, c, d, e, f, g and h were detected through transits, among others with the TRAPPIST-South telescope at La Silla, the Very Large Telescope and the Spitzer Space Telescope. Three planets had already been reported in early 2016.

The star has only 8 percent of the Sun's mass. The orbits are not much larger than the system of Jupiter's Galilean moons. Three planets lie in the habitable zone; according to the density measurements, at least the inner six are probably rocky.

Source: ESO: "Ultracool Dwarf and the Seven Planets", 2017-02-22
