Kepler-444: five small planets around an 11.2-billion-year-old star

The star Kepler-444 is 11.2 billion years old: it formed when the universe had reached less than a fifth of its present age. It is orbited by five planets whose sizes range between those of Mercury and Venus. No older system of Earth-sized planets was known at the time; it is about 2.5 times the age of Earth.

All five planets orbit the star in less than ten days. The age was derived from tiny variations in the star's brightness. The system is about 117 light-years away.

Source: NASA/JPL: "Astronomers Discover Ancient System with Five Small Planets.", 2015-01-28
