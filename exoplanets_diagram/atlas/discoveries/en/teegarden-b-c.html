June 18, 2019

Teegarden's Star: two Earth-like planets 12.5 light-years away

An international team led by the University of Göttingen discovered two planets around Teegarden's Star as part of the CARMENES project. The star is only about 12.5 light-years away, roughly 2,700 degrees Celsius hot, and has about one-tenth of the Sun's mass. It was not discovered until 2003.

According to the team, both planets are only slightly heavier than Earth and lie in the habitable zone, where water can be liquid. No transits have been observed. Conversely, seen from Teegarden's Star the planets of the solar system pass in front of the Sun.

Source: Universität Göttingen: "Press release: View of the Earth in front of the Sun." (No. 116), 2019-06-18
