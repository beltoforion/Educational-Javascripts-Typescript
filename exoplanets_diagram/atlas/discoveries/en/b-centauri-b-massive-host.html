b Centauri b: a planet around a very massive and hot pair of stars

ESO's Very Large Telescope has directly imaged a planet in the binary star system b Centauri. The pair of stars has at least six solar masses – at the time of the announcement the most massive and hottest system around which a planet had been confirmed. Until then, no planet was known around a star with more than three solar masses.

The planet has about ten Jupiter masses and orbits the pair at 100 times the distance of Jupiter from the Sun – one of the widest orbits known. The system, visible to the naked eye, lies about 325 light-years away in the constellation Centaurus.

Source: ESO: "ESO telescope images planet around most massive star pair to date.", 2021-12-08
