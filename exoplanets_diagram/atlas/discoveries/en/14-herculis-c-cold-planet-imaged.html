14 Herculis c: one of the coldest exoplanets ever imaged

The James Webb Space Telescope has directly imaged the planet 14 Herculis c. With a temperature of about minus 3 degrees Celsius, it is one of the coldest exoplanets ever imaged. Its star is 60 light-years away.

The planet has about seven Jupiter masses and orbits its star on a highly elliptical path at a distance of about 2.25 billion kilometers. It is fainter than expected for its age of about four billion years and its mass. The researchers suspect complex processes in its atmosphere as the cause.
