October 1, 2024

Four small planets around Barnard's Star

Barnard's Star is a red dwarf only six light-years away. After the Alpha Centauri system, it is the star closest to the Sun. In October 2024, a team using the ESPRESSO spectrograph of the Very Large Telescope detected a planet here that is considerably lighter than Earth and orbits its star in just 3.15 days.

A little later, in March 2025, measurements with the spectrograph MAROON-X at the Gemini North telescope confirmed this planet and found three more. All four planets were discovered with the radial velocity method. They have only about 20 to 30 percent of Earth's mass and orbit their star so closely that they are unsuitable for life.
