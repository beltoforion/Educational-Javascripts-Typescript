51 Eridani b: discovery with the Gemini Planet Imager

Using the Gemini Planet Imager (GPI) of the Gemini Observatory, astronomers discovered a young planet around the star 51 Eridani. The near-infrared discovery image of December 18, 2014 shows the planet as a dot below the masked star; it is a million times fainter than the star.

According to the Gemini Observatory the planet has about two Jupiter masses and orbits the star, which is about 100 light-years away and only 20 million years old, at 13 times the Earth–Sun distance. Methane was detected in its atmosphere; the temperature is about 430 degrees Celsius.
