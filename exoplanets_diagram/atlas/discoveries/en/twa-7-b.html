Webb directly images a possible planet with the mass of Saturn

Using the coronagraph of its MIRI instrument, the James Webb Space Telescope has directly imaged an object in the debris disk around the young star TWA 7 that is very likely a planet. TWA 7 b would be the first exoplanet that JWST itself has discovered in this way. With roughly the mass of Saturn, it would also be the lightest exoplanet directly imaged so far. It is located in a gap in one of the dust rings around the star, which is about 111 light-years away, exactly where a planet of this mass shaping the debris disk was expected.
