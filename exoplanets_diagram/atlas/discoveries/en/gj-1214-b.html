GJ 1214 b: a super-Earth with a thick atmosphere

The MEarth project discovered GJ 1214 b when the planet passed in front of its small star, only 40 light-years away. ESO's HARPS spectrograph was used to determine its mass: about six Earth masses at 2.7 Earth radii. After CoRoT-7b, it was the second super-Earth with a known mass and radius.

Because the measured radius is larger than models predict, the researchers inferred a thick atmosphere about 200 kilometers deep, the first found around a super-Earth. The planet orbits its star every 38 hours and its surface is probably about 200 degrees Celsius hot.

Source: ESO: "Astronomers Find World with Thick, Inhospitable Atmosphere and an Icy Heart", 2009-12-16
