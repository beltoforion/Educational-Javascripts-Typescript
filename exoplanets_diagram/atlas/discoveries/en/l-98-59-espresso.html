L 98-59: a rocky planet with half the mass of Venus

Using the ESPRESSO spectrograph on ESO's Very Large Telescope, the planets of the star L 98-59, 35 light-years away, were measured. The innermost has only half the mass of Venus – the lightest exoplanet whose mass had been determined with the radial velocity technique up to that time.

The two innermost planets are probably dry. For the third, up to 30 percent of the mass could be water. The team also found a fourth planet and hints of a fifth in the habitable zone, which was not yet confirmed at that time.

Source: ESO: "New ESO observations show rocky exoplanet has just half the mass of Venus." (eso2112), 2021-08-05
