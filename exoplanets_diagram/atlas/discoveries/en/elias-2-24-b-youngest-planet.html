Elias 2-24 b: the youngest planet known so far

Elias 2-24 b is less than a million years old, making it the youngest planet detected so far. The previous record holders – two planets each around PDS 70 and WISPIT 2 – are more than five million years old. The planet is still gathering material from the disk around its star, about 450 light-years away.

It was found in archival data from the Keck Observatory taken in 2018 and 2020; observations with the VLT and ALMA support the finding. It lies in a gap in the disk. Its mass is still uncertain, with figures ranging from about one to four Jupiter masses. According to the team, the object is at the detection limit of current technology.
