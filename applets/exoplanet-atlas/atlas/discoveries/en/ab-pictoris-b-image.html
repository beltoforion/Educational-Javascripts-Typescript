Companion of AB Pictoris on the border between planet and brown dwarf

The NACO instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope imaged a faint companion next to the young star AB Pictoris. In the coronagraphic image from March 2003 the star is hidden behind a mask; the companion appears as a red dot at the lower left.

Observations over a year and a half showed that it is not a background object. Model calculations gave 13 to 14 Jupiter masses – right on the border between a massive planet and a brown dwarf. AB Pictoris is about 150 light-years away; the companion is about nine times farther from it than Neptune is from the Sun.

Sources: ESO: "Yes, it is the Image of an Exoplanet. Astronomers Confirm the First Image of a Planet Outside of Our Solar System" (eso0515; contains the section on AB Pictoris)., 2005-04-30; ESO: "The star AB Pictoris and its companion" (image eso0515d)., 2005-04-30
