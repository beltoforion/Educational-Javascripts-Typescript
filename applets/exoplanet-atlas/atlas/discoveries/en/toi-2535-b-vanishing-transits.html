TOI-2535 b: a planet whose transits have vanished

The hot Jupiter TOI-2535 b orbits its star, about 1,000 light-years away, in 8.25 days. The TESS satellite observed the system between April 2019 and March 2026. Over the years the transits became shorter and shallower and arrived up to six hours early or late – in 2026 they failed to appear at all.

The planet has not disappeared: according to the analysis of a team led by the University of California, Los Angeles, the gravity of a so far unseen neighboring planet is tilting its orbit, so that it no longer passes in front of the star as seen from Earth. Transits are expected to be visible again in 30 to 50 years.

Source: University of California, Los Angeles (via EurekAlert): "First known 'disappearing planet' has a neighbor that makes it wobble like a top.", 2026-11-09
