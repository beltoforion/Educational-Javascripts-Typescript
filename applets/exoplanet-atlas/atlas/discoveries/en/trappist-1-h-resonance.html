Orbital period of TRAPPIST-1 h confirmed: the planets form a resonant chain

For the outermost planet, TRAPPIST-1 h, there was initially only an estimate of the orbital period. From the regular pattern in the orbital periods of the six inner planets, an orbital resonance, a team calculated six possible values, only one of which was compatible with the existing data. Observations with the Kepler space telescope confirmed it: the planet orbits its star every 19 days.

TRAPPIST-1 h lies beyond the outer edge of the habitable zone and is probably too cold for life as we know it. According to the researchers, the resonant chain suggests that the planets migrated inward together.

Source: NASA/JPL: "Astronomers Confirm Orbital Details of TRAPPIST-1h.", 2017-05-22
