Supposed planet HD 131399 Ab turns out to be a background star

In 2016 a team using the SPHERE instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope reported a directly imaged planet in the triple-star system HD 131399, about 320 light-years away. The composite image shows the three stars and the point of light that was interpreted as the planet HD 131399 Ab, with about four Jupiter masses.

ESO amended the announcement in April 2022: observations over the following years showed that the object is not a planet but a background star.

Sources: ESO: "A Surprising Planet with Three Suns" (eso1624)., 2016-07-07; ESO: "SPHERE observations of the planet HD 131399Ab" (image eso1624d, with the edit note of 19 April 2022)., 2016-07-07
