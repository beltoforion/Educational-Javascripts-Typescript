TOI-561 b: a rocky lava world that seems to have an atmosphere

The James Webb Space Telescope has measured the dayside of the ultra-hot super-Earth TOI-561 b. As a bare rock it should be almost 2,700 degrees Celsius hot, but the measurements indicate closer to 1,800 degrees. In the team's view this is best explained by a thick atmosphere of volatile substances above a global magma ocean.

TOI-561 b has about 1.4 times the radius of Earth and orbits its star in less than 11 hours. The star is about twice as old as the Sun and poor in iron. NASA calls this the most convincing sign so far that a rocky planet beyond the Solar System has an atmosphere. The exact composition is still open.
