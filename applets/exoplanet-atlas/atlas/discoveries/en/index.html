Our Solar System

The eight planets of the Sun for comparison.

  • Mercury: small, hot and airless
  • Venus: Earth-sized, but hostile to life
  • Earth: the yardstick for Earth-like worlds
  • Mars: a cold desert at the edge of the habitable zone
  • Jupiter: the measure of giant planets
  • Saturn: the ringed planet of low density
  • Uranus: the tilted ice giant
  • Neptune: distant ice giant and namesake of the mini-Neptunes
