Planet candidate at the base of a spiral arm around HD 135344B

Using the ERIS instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope, astronomers discovered a possible forming planet in the disk around the young star HD 135344B. In the image a coronagraph hides the star (black circle); a white circle marks the candidate at the base of one of the disk's spiral arms.

ESO describes the candidate as about twice the size of Jupiter and roughly as far from its star as Neptune is from the Sun. The star lies about 440 light-years away. This is a candidate, not a confirmed discovery.
