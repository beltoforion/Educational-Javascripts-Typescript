HIP 65426 b: Webb's first direct image of an exoplanet

On September 1, 2022, the first direct image of an exoplanet taken with the James Webb Space Telescope was published. It shows the gas giant HIP 65426 b in four infrared filters of the NIRCam and MIRI instruments, whose coronagraphs block out the starlight. In the near-infrared, the planet is more than 10,000 times fainter than its star.

HIP 65426 b had been discovered in 2017 with the SPHERE instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope. It has about six to eight Jupiter masses, is only 15 to 20 million years old, and is about 100 times farther from its star than Earth is from the Sun. The system is about 385 light-years away.

Sources: ESA/Webb: "Webb Takes Its First Exoplanet Image", 2022-09-01; ESO: "ESO’s SPHERE Unveils its First Exoplanet", 2017-07-06
