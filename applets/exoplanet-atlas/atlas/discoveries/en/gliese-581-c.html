Gliese 581 c: super-Earth reported in the habitable zone

Using ESO's HARPS spectrograph, a team reported a planet of about five Earth masses orbiting the red dwarf Gliese 581, which is 20.5 light-years away. Gliese 581 c orbits the star in 13 days and was the smallest exoplanet known up to that time.

According to the estimate made at the time, the mean temperature was between 0 and 40 degrees Celsius; water could therefore be liquid. ESO spoke of the first Earth-like planet in a habitable zone. The radius was not measured but estimated from models at 1.5 times Earth's radius. There was also strong evidence for a third planet of about eight Earth masses.

Source: ESO: "Astronomers Find First Earth-like Planet in Habitable Zone." (eso0722), 2007-04-25
