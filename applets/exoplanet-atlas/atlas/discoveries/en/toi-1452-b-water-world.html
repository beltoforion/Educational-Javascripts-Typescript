TOI-1452 b: a super-Earth that could be a water world

Using NASA's TESS satellite and ground-based telescopes, a team led by the University of Montreal discovered a super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf about 100 light-years away. TOI-1452 b is about 70 percent larger than Earth, roughly five times as massive, and orbits the star every 11 days.

Its density would be consistent with a very deep ocean: in one model calculation, water makes up as much as 30 percent of the mass. This is not established. The planet could equally be a huge rock with little or no atmosphere, or a rocky planet with an envelope of hydrogen and helium.

Source: NASA: "Discovery Alert: Intriguing New 'Super-Earth' Could Get a Closer Look.", 2022-08-24
