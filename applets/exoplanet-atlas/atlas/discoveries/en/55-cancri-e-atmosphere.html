Hints of an atmosphere around a rocky planet

Observations with the James Webb Space Telescope provide the best evidence so far for an atmosphere around a rocky planet outside the Solar System. The super-Earth 55 Cancri e is about twice the size of Earth and orbits its star, 41 light-years away, so closely that its surface is probably covered by an ocean of magma. The measurements point to an atmosphere that is rich in carbon dioxide or carbon monoxide and could be continuously replenished from the magma.

Source: ESA/Webb: "Webb hints at possible atmosphere surrounding rocky exoplanet." Online; esawebb.org; 2024-05-08
