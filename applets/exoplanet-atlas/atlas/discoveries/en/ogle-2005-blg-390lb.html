OGLE-2005-BLG-390Lb: a cool super-Earth found by microlensing

A worldwide network of telescopes, including the Danish 1.54-meter telescope at La Silla, discovered the planet through gravitational microlensing: the gravity of a foreground star briefly magnifies the light of a distant star, and a planet reveals itself through a small additional deviation.

The planet has about five Earth masses, a value uncertain by a factor of two, and was at the time the least massive planet known around an ordinary star. It orbits a red dwarf star in about ten years, roughly 20,000 light-years away near the center of the Milky Way. Its surface temperature is estimated at minus 220 degrees Celsius.

Source: ESO: "It's Far, It's Small, It's Cool: It's an Icy Exoplanet!", 2006-01-25
